ಸಂತೆ = market / open-air or street bazaar  
ಒಳಗೊಂದು = one inside  
ಮನೆ = house  
ಮಾಡಿ = build  
ಶಬ್ದ = noise  
ನಾಚಿದೊಡೆ = be shy of  
ಎಂತಯ್ಯ = how can you

"How can you be shy of / annoyed by noise or commotion if you've built your house right in the middle of a market?"

This is a line from one of my favorite _vachanas_ <Fn id="vachana" /> by [Akka Mahadevi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akka_Mahadevi)

The whole ವಚನ goes:  
ಬೆಟ್ಟದ ಮೇಲೊಂದು ಮನೆಯ ಮಾಡಿ, ಮೃಗಗಳಿಗಂಜಿದೊಡೆಂತಯ್ಯ?  
ಸಮುದ್ರದ ತಡಿಯಲೊಂದು ಮನೆಯ ಮಾಡಿ, ನೊರೆತೆರೆಗಳಿಗಂಜಿದೊಡೆಂತಯ್ಯ?  
ಸಂತೆಯೊಳಗೊಂದು ಮನೆಯ ಮಾಡಿ, ಶಬ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ನಾಚಿದೊಡೆಂತಯ್ಯ?  
ಚೆನ್ನಮಲ್ಲಿಕಾರ್ಜುನ ಕೇಳಯ್ಯ ನೀ  
ಲೋಕದೊಳಗೆ ಹುಟ್ಟಿದ ಬಳಿಕ ಸ್ತುತಿ ನಿಂದೆಗಳು ಬಂದಡೆ  
ಮನದಲ್ಲಿ ಕೋಪವ ತಾಳದೆ ಸಮಾಧಾನಿಯಾಗಿರಬೇಕು

## My interpretation

The theme of this vachana is stoicism and acceptance IMO. It talks of how you should be able to control your emotions come what may, once you are born in this world.

The particular line I've highlighted is also about acceptance of trade-offs.

- You want the convenience of a house amidst a market, but cannot handle the noise?
- You want to say something controversial, but cannot take the heat?
- You want the freedom to do whatever you please, but don't want to deal with the consequences?
- You want to be loved for who you are, but don't want to accept others for who they are?

**You can't have your cake and eat it too!**

You cannot unfortunately have it both ways! There IS a cost to things and you often cannot pick only the part that's convenient for you.

That leads to the next part of this Vachana: well, you're born here. Shit happens. There'll be praise, there'll be criticism, there'll be disagreement, there'll be misunderstanding, and there'll be appreciation.

And often, some are deserved, some aren't.

You got to be able to go on without reacting all too much to these things.  
You cannot really control the world around you. But what you CAN is how YOU react to the it.

You've built your house right in the middle of a market. Maybe stop complaining / whining about it?

## ಸ್ಥಿತಪ್ರಜ್ಞತೆ

ಸ್ಥಿತ = stable / steady / firm  
ಪ್ರಜ್ಞತೆ = wisdom / intellect

I'd say this is the closest word for the concept of Stoicism in Kannada.  
Describes one's ability to be composed with steadfast wisdom, to be free from fickle-mindedness and intense desires - no matter the external stimuli.

Many many great philosophers have explored this concept and have their own take on how to achieve it.  
IMO it often boils down to practising letting go of "अहम्"<Fn id="aham" />.

## My take

Ok here's the thing.  
To achieve "equanimity", you have to practise Vairāgya, an extreme form of detachment.

To be human is to err.  
To be human is to feel.  
To be human is to suffer.

It's simply impossible NOT to react to things.  
With practice, yes, you can try and be indifferent to things happening around you, but through that you lose a part of yourself.

So, go forth, cry, laugh, and I guess, be you?
